Travel with the calendar

Seasonal destinations

Match the timing of your trip to places that shine during that part of the year.

Spring destinations

Kyoto, Paris and Washington D.C. work well for blossoms, city walks and comfortable outdoor days.

Temperature10°C–20°C
ActivitiesCherry blossoms, cafés, walking tours
ClothingLight jackets, layers, walking shoes
Travel moodCultural · Relaxing

Summer destinations

Santorini, the Amalfi Coast and Bali shine when the plan is built around water, sailing and long evenings.

Temperature25°C–35°C
ActivitiesBeach hopping, sailing, swimming
ClothingLight cotton, swimwear, sunglasses
Travel moodRelaxing · Luxury

Autumn destinations

New England, Bavaria and Kyoto offer foliage, hiking and a quieter pace than high summer.

Temperature5°C–15°C
ActivitiesFoliage viewing, wine tasting, hiking
ClothingSweaters, boots, light jackets
Travel moodNature · Cultural

Winter destinations

Banff and the Swiss Alps are ideal for snow sports, while Maldives offers a warmer winter-sun alternative.

Temperature-10°C–5°C in snowy regions
ActivitiesSkiing, snowboarding, thermal baths
ClothingHeavy coat, thermal layers, snow boots
Travel moodAdventure · Relaxing
